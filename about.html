About Adventure MobileCade

Adventure MobileCade is a locally owned mobile gaming startup based in Platte City, Missouri. We bring curated gaming experiences to communities across the KC metro.

Services We Provide

Mobile Gaming Stations — Classic + modern video game setups for all ages

Gaming Library — NES, SNES, N64, GameCube, Wii, and more

Modern Multiplayer Games — Switch, Xbox, and party‑friendly titles

Event Attraction Setup — Clean, self‑contained, plug‑and‑play presentation

Family‑Friendly Entertainment — Safe, inclusive fun for kids, teens, and adults

Festival & Community Event Support — Ideal for city celebrations, Main Street events, and local gatherings

Private Party Packages — Birthdays, neighborhood parties, and apartment complex events

Youth Program Engagement — Great for libraries, Parks & Rec, and after‑school programs

Nostalgia Experiences — “Decades of Gaming! One Epic Adventure!” themed setups

Our Mission

We believe gaming brings people together. Our goal is to create accessible, nostalgic, and community-focused entertainment.

How We Operate

Every activation is fully self-contained with modular stations, helper-friendly workflow cards, and clear labeling.
